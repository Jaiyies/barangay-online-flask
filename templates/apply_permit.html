Terms and Conditions

1. Acceptance of Terms
By submitting this application, you agree to comply with all barangay rules and regulations regarding events and gatherings.

2. Event Guidelines
All events must adhere to the following:

  • Maximum of 300 attendees per event
  • Start Time: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • End Time: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Proper waste management must be observed
  • No excessive noise beyond barangay limits
  • Event must be 10 days before the event date

3. Responsibility
The applicant is fully responsible for the event and must ensure the safety and orderliness of the activity.

4. Data Privacy
Your information will be used solely for permit processing and barangay records in accordance with the Data Privacy Act.

5. Cancellation Policy
Permit must be cancelled at least 3 days before the event. Failure to do so may result in penalties.
