About Us

Get to know your Barangay Sto. Niño Online Services Portal

Welcome to Barangay Sto. Niño Online Services Portal

Our goal is to make the process of obtaining documents and submitting permit applications easier, faster, and more reliable for all residents of Barangay Sto. Niño.

Through this online portal, you no longer have to wait in long lines at the barangay hall. You can request documents and apply for permits right from the comfort of your home.

Our Mission

To provide efficient, transparent, and accessible government services to every resident of Barangay Sto. Niño through digital innovation and good governance.

Our Vision

A progressive, united, and digitally-empowered barangay where every citizen has easy access to quality public service.

Services Offered

Barangay Clearance

For employment, business, and other transactions.

Certificate of Indigency

For medical and financial assistance.

Certificate of Residency

Proof of residence in the barangay.

Community Event Permits

For private occasions and activities.
