Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

1 How do I change my password?

If you are on the Login page:

  • Click "Forgot Password?" below the login fields.
  • Enter your registered email address and your desired new password.
  • Make sure all information is correct before confirming the password change.

If you are already logged in:

  • Click your Profile in the upper-right corner of the screen.
  • Select the option to Change Password.
  • Enter your registered email address and desired new password.
  • Make sure all information is correct before confirming.
2 Can I change my email address?

No. Your email address is permanent once your account has been created and cannot be changed.

3 Can I delete my account?

No. Account deletion is currently not available.

4 What should I do if my account has been hacked?

If you believe that your account has been hacked or accessed without your permission, contact the Barangay Sto. Niño Help Desk for assistance.

Contact Number: 0992 225 0927

Office Hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday

Excluding holidays and special non-working days.

5 What is this website for?

The Barangay Sto. Niño Online Services website makes common barangay services easier to access online.

Residents can use the website to:

  • Request Barangay Clearance
  • Request Certificate of Indigency
  • Request Proof of Residency
  • Apply for Community Event Permits
  • Track the status of their requests
Note: The website does not currently include online payments, payroll services, health records, or other barangay services outside its current scope.
6 How do I apply for a Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, or Proof of Residency?
  • From your Dashboard, go to Quick Actions.
  • Click "Request Document."
  • Select the document you want to request.
  • Fill out all required information.
  • If requesting Proof of Residency, review the additional requirements provided.
  • Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions by checking the required boxes.
  • Review all the information you entered and make sure it is correct.
  • Click "Submit Request."
  • Take note of your Reference Number / Tracking Number.
Note: You can also view your request and reference number on the My Request page.
7 Can a minor apply for a Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, or Proof of Residency?

The website is intended for residents of Barangay Sto. Niño who are 18 years old and above.

For document requests involving minors, please visit the Barangay Sto. Niño Office for assistance.

Office Hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday

Excluding holidays and special non-working days.

8 What is an Area Representative?

An Area Representative is a person who represents or serves as a contact person for residents in a particular area or community within the barangay.

For example, an HOA President may serve as an Area Representative for residents in their community.

9 How do I get the signature of the Area Representative?
  • Identify and contact your Area Representative.
  • Prepare a clean piece of paper containing the following:
    • Area Representative's printed name
    • Signature
    • Date of signature
  • Take a clear photo of the signed document.
  • Upload the photo to the Document Request page.
Note: Please refer to the sample image provided on the website for the correct format.
10 Who can be my reference person?

Your reference person:

  • Must not be a relative.
  • Should preferably be a resident of Barangay Sto. Niño.
11 How long must I have lived in Barangay Sto. Niño to request a Proof of Residency?

To request a Proof of Residency, you must have been a resident of Barangay Sto. Niño for at least 12 months (1 year) at the time of your request.

12 What if I do not have a Precinct Number?

If you do not have a Precinct Number, please consult a Barangay staff member for assistance on how to proceed.

You may visit the Barangay Office during:

Office Hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday

Excluding holidays and special non-working days.

14 How do I claim my Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Indigency, or Proof of Residency?
  • Go to the My Request page.
  • Find the document you requested and check its status.
  • Once the status is marked "Approved," you may claim the document at the Barangay Office.
  • Present your Reference Number and verify your personal details.
15 Where can I find my Reference Number?
  • Go to the My Request page.
  • Find the document you want to check.
  • Click "View."
Note: Your Reference Number, along with other request details, will be displayed.
16 How do I apply for an Event Permit?
  • From your Dashboard, go to Quick Actions.
  • Click "Apply for Permit."
  • Fill out all required information.
  • If you are applying on behalf of a company or organization, provide a valid ID as proof of identity and supporting documents related to the event.
  • Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions by checking the required boxes.
  • Review all the information you entered and make sure it is correct.
  • Click "Submit Application."
Note: Examples of supporting documents include:
  • Authorization letter
  • Company ID
  • Event proposal
  • Other documents showing your connection to the company or organization
Note: It is recommended to submit an event permit application at least 10 days before the event date to help avoid scheduling conflicts.
17 How can I track the status of my request or application?

Go to the My Request page to view your submitted requests and check their current status.

18 Where can I check venue availability?

You can check the availability of barangay venues and courts on the Events Calendar page.
